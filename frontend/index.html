Follow the settled work

  1. Stake Sepolia ETH on YES or NO before the window starts.
  2. After the window ends, anyone can resolve once Core certifies its final round.
  3. Winners claim their share of both pools. There is no fee.

One-sided markets refund after betting closes. Unresolved markets refund 30 days after the window ends, so a late Reporter can still certify the result.

Core is an external, upgradeable source. A changed implementation prevents resolution; the timeout still allows refunds.
